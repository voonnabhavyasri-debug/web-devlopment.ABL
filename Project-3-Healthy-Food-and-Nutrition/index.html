WHY FOOD CHOICES MATTER

Build a balanced plate

A variety of foods can provide nutrients the body needs. Aim for balance and variety rather than perfection.

🥕

Fruits & vegetables

Add different colours and varieties to meals when available.

🌾

Grains & energy

Rice, oats, and whole grains can be part of balanced meals.

🥜

Protein foods

Beans, lentils, eggs, dairy, nuts, or other foods can add protein.

SIMPLE INSPIRATION

Easy food ideas

Fresh veggie bowl

Mix seasonal vegetables with grains and a protein food you enjoy.

Fruit breakfast

Try fruit with yoghurt, oats, or another breakfast you like.

Warm soup

Vegetable or lentil soup can be a comforting meal or side dish.

EVERYDAY WELLNESS

Healthy habits to practise

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Include a variety of foods across meals.
  • Eat regular meals that suit your routine.
  • Wash fruits and vegetables before preparing them.
  • Enjoy food without labelling foods as “good” or “bad”.

“A balanced routine is built one everyday choice at a time.”
